A Note For You

An envelope full of love

To my Madam Jii,

You became home to my heart before life even gave me the chance to hold your hand

Sometimes I sit alone and wonder how all this happened. How did a girl I had never even met become the one I think about every single day? How did someone I never sat beside become someone whose absence I can feel so strongly? And still, somehow, you became one of the realest parts of my life.

People talk about love as if it only becomes true after dates, long drives, and shared places. But with you, it started much deeper than that. I fell for your heart, your softness, your trust, and the peace I felt just by knowing you were there. Even one small notification from you could change my whole day.

I have never heard your voice beside me, sat next to you on a bench, walked with you on a quiet evening, or looked into your eyes while speaking face to face. Yet none of that made you feel distant to my heart. You became a comfort in my days, a thought in my prayers, and the person I quietly hope is okay before I sleep.

There were times I wanted to tell you a hundred things but held them back because I never wanted my feelings to feel heavy in your life. Still, you should know how much your trust means to me. It is not something money, gifts, or big words can replace. To be trusted by you is one of the softest, most precious feelings I know.

You changed things in me quietly. Not because you demanded it, but because your happiness began to matter to me more than my old habits, my anger, and my ego. I never saw that as sacrifice. I saw it as becoming better in the direction of someone I deeply love.

Somewhere along the way, I started wishing for your wins as sincerely as I wish for my own. I want life to be gentle with you. I want you to have peace, comfort, freedom, and every little reason to smile. Even reading Ramraksha feels more meaningful now, because the people we care for become part of the prayers we keep.

Distance and silence taught me something I did not understand at first: a passing crush can fade, but some people become such a real part of you that even time cannot make them feel ordinary. You are that person for me, Madam Jii. You made my heart feel connected in a way I never thought possible.

Maybe life will take its own time. Maybe one day all of this will turn into a real day, a real meeting, a real memory I can touch. Until then, I want you to know this: I do not need you to be perfect, and I do not expect you to owe me anything. I only want to be someone who cares for you honestly. If life gave me a thousand chances to know you again, I would still choose you every single time.

And if someday everything else fades, one truth still will not: I loved you with every part of my heart, long before the world gave us perfect timing. Whatever shape our story takes, you will always be a beautiful part of mine. My Madam Jii, Meri Butki, thank you for becoming so special to me.

Forever yours,
Om
